# Tutorials

Lessons for getting the feel of the library. They take one controlled path and
are meant to be followed start to finish, in order, without substitutions.

- [Build your first model](first-model.md) — create an EnergyPlus model from
  nothing, watch a rename rewrite the references that point at it, write it to
  disk, and read it back.

When you know your way around and want to get something specific done, the
[how-to guides](../how-to/index.md) are the shorter route.
